# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SmartFill to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing others' private information (such as addresses or emails) without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **himanshuch8055@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that violate this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, depending on the severity and whether the behavior is repeated.
